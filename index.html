MNU DATA CENTER - Preliminary Acceptance & Handover

Major Milestone Achieved: Preliminary Handover of the State-of-the-Art MNU DATA CENTER!

I am thrilled to announce the successful completion and preliminary acceptance of our new enterprise-grade Data Center facility. This critical milestone represents months of meticulous engineering, infrastructure deployment, and rigorous testing to build a robust, scalable, and highly available core foundation.

Key Infrastructure & Technical Highlights:

  • Rack Deployment & Cabling Excellence: High-density rack enclosures with structured cable routing, optical fiber backbones, and color-coded patch panels for optimal thermal management.
  • Precision Cooling & Environmental Control: Smart HVAC precision cooling units maintaining strict humidity and temperature thresholds for peak server efficiency.
  • Power Redundancy & UPS Systems: Dual-bus power distribution, redundant UPS battery banks, and automatic transfer switches ensuring 99.999% uptime reliability.
  • Zero-Trust Security & Access Control: Multi-factor physical access control, biometric authentication, and 24/7 CCTV surveillance integration.
  • Site Acceptance Testing (SAT): Rigorous load testing, thermal imaging audits, and seamless emergency power cutover simulations.

Deep gratitude to the engineering teams, contractors, and leadership whose collaborative dedication made this milestone possible. Ready to power the next generation of digital services!

WE Data Center & Network Infrastructure Lab

Enterprise Data Center Infrastructure & Advanced Network Topologies

Comprehensive inspection and technical overview of Telecom Egypt (WE) enterprise data center facility, structured server rack deployments, high-performance optical fiber distribution, and simulated network topologies using Cisco Packet Tracer for routing protocol validation and traffic optimization.

Key Infrastructure & Technical Highlights:

  • High-Density Server Racks: Industrial server cabinets with structured cable management, color-coded patch cords, and optical patch panels for optimal thermal management.
  • Power Distribution & Backup: Redundant dual-feed power distribution units (PDUs) with real-time telemetry and industrial UPS protection.
  • Cisco Network Architecture: Multi-tier core/distribution/access switching topologies, inter-VLAN routing, OSPF protocol convergence, and robust ACL security policies.
  • Zero-Trust Monitoring: Continuous link monitoring, packet inspection with Wireshark, and physical access verification.
